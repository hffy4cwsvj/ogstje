Narcolepsy is a chronic neurological sleep disorder that causes excessive daytime sleepiness, sudden sleep attacks, and disruptions to daily activities. People with narcolepsy may struggle with work, school, driving, and social life, often experiencing a reduced quality of life if the condition is left unmanaged.

Explanation

Narcolepsy is a condition that makes people feel very sleepy during the day, even if they have had a good night's sleep. It can cause sudden sleep attacks, where a person falls asleep without warning. This can make it hard to do everyday things like going to school or work, driving, or even having fun with friends.

[Narcolepsy Warning Why You](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/)

How Narcolepsy Affects Daily Life:

Daytime Sleepiness: People with narcolepsy often feel extremely sleepy during the day, which can make it hard to concentrate or stay awake during activities.

Sudden Sleep Attacks: These can happen at any time, even during important activities, causing disruptions.

Impact on School and Work: The need for naps and the sudden sleepiness can make it difficult to keep up with tasks and responsibilities.

Social and Emotional Effects: It can be frustrating and embarrassing, affecting friendships and social life.
