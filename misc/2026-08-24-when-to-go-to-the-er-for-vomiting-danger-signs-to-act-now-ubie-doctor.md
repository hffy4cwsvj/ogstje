Vomiting after a head injury—especially repeated vomiting—is a warning sign of a potentially serious brain injury, such as a concussion, skull fracture, or internal bleeding. Anyone who vomits after hitting their head should seek emergency medical care right away to rule out life-threatening complications.

Because head injuries can worsen quickly and symptoms aren't always obvious, it's critical to act fast. If you're unsure how serious your symptoms are, take a free, instant, online symptom check to better understand what may be happening and get clear guidance on your next steps.
