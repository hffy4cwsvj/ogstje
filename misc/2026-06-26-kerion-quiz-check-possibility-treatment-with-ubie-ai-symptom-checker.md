What is Kerion?

A kerion is an abscess that appears as a large, spongy lump filled with pus and caused by inflammation from a fungal infection. It is commonly seen on the scalp but can develop anywhere. Anyone can be affected, but it is most common in children. Infection can spread through physical contact, sharing hairbrushes, towels, bedding, etc.

Typical Symptoms of Kerion

Hair loss

Pus-filled blisters or bumps on the skin

Area of bald skin with unclear edges

Dry and flaky skin
