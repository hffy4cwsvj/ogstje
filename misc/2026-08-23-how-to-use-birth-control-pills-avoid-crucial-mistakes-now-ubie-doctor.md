Birth control pills are a daily oral contraceptive used to prevent pregnancy. They work in two main ways: by suppressing ovulation (stopping the ovaries from releasing an egg) and by thickening cervical mucus to block sperm from reaching an egg. For maximum effectiveness—up to 99% with perfect use—the pill must be taken at the same time every day. Missing a dose or taking it inconsistently can significantly reduce its protection.

Birth control pills are a type of oral contraceptive used to prevent pregnancy. They work mainly by stopping the ovaries from releasing eggs, a process known as ovulation. Additionally, these pills make the mucus in the cervix thicker, which helps block sperm from reaching any eggs that may be released.

How to Use Birth Control Pills:

[Use Birth Control](https://nootropic-atlas.escortskhi.com/blog/7383807751.html)

Daily Routine: Take one pill every day, ideally at the same time each day. This helps maintain the hormone levels necessary to prevent pregnancy.

Starting the Pill: Depending on the type of pill, you may start taking it on the first day of your period or the first Sunday after your period begins. Follow the specific instructions provided with your prescription.

Consistency is Key: It's important to take the pill consistently without missing doses. Missing pills can increase the risk of pregnancy.
