Understanding Safety

Efficacy

A few of the potential health concerns related to Naltrexone include depression, hypertension, and seizures. This medication is intended for adults and should never be given to adolescents or individuals with uncontrolled high blood pressure.

The daily dosage of Naltrexone should not exceed two tablets per day. A physician will provide a dosage schedule that caters to the needs of each patient. Naltrexone is generally safe for most adults without existing health conditions that can cause additional problems.

Naltrexone can be effective in the right circumstances, but like any drug, patients should be cautious to avoid drug abuse at all costs. Considering Naltrexone is an opioid receptor antagonist, it’s associated with acute opioid misuse and withdrawal.
