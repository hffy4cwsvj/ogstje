How to Do the Aesthetics Superset

Set a timer to go off every four minutes.

Perform 8 to 12 reps of the dumbbell incline bench press, then perform 3 to 5 reps of the barbell deadlift. Rest until the timer goes off.

That’s 1 round. Do 5 rounds.

For a more metabolic workout, choose lighter weights and set your timer to go off every two or three minutes.
