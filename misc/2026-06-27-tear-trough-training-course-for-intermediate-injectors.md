About this lesson

Refine your skills in tear trough rejuvenation with this Level B training course focused on treating hollowing, mid-face volume loss, and under-eye fatigue.

In this lesson, Dr Juan Sopena begins with a comprehensive assessment of the tear troughs and mid-face region, identifying fat pockets, structural deficits, and areas requiring ultra-deep support. Using a “lifter” product for foundational correction, he demonstrates precise placement techniques to minimize discomfort while maximizing durability. Throughout the procedure, he applies strategic molding, blending, and density control to achieve seamless, natural contouring and visible under-eye improvement.

Why Take this Course?

This course is ideal for intermediate injectors seeking to refine their under-eye and mid-face rejuvenation techniques. You’ll learn to address hollowing, visible fat pockets, and asymmetries with precision while mastering blending and molding methods for smooth, natural results. The training also emphasizes combining tear trough correction with mid-face support to achieve balanced, harmonious outcomes.

Learning Outcomes:

• Perform a structured assessment of the tear troughs, mid-face support, and fat pads

• Apply Teosyal PureSense Ultra Deep filler using strategic placement and density control for natural rejuvenation

• Mold and blend the product effectively to create a smooth, refreshed under-eye contour
