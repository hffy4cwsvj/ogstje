When Anderson Silva speaks, you can hear him smile. There’s joy behind every word he says, right from the hearty, “Hello, my friend!” with which he started our conversation.

His joy makes perfect sense: the 43-year-old champion has a lot to celebrate these days. From 2006 to 2013, he reigned atop the UFC’s middleweight division, cementing his legacy as one of the greatest competitors the promotion has ever seen. UFC President Dana White and Joe Rogan have referred to Silva as one of the best fighters to ever enter the Octagon.

In 2017, Silva's legacy was threatened when he was pulled from a scheduled bout with Kelvin Gastelum due to a failed drug test, the second in his career. According to a statement from the United States Anti-Doping Administration (USADA) at the time, Silva tested positive for two banned substances, methyltestosterone and a diuretic, which could potentially have led to a competition ban of up to four years.
