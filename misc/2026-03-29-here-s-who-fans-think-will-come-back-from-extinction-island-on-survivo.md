# Here's Who Fans Think Will Come Back From Extinction Island on 'Survivor'

This season of Survivor brought a major twist in the form of Extinction Island. When castaways are voted out, they have the option of heading to the Edge of Extinction, where they can wait around on a desolate beach for the chance to get back in the game.

Fans were also excited about the return of four former players: Aubry, David, Joe, and Kelley. Of course, these four were an instant target in the game, and with last week's blindside of Kelley, all the returnees are now on Extinction Island—along with first-time players Chris, Eric, Julia, and Reem.

Prediction website Gold Derby asked people to vote on which veteran player has the best shot at re-gaining a shot at the million-dollar prize and title of Sole Survivor. Here are the results. (Note: This survey only looked at the four returning players, but it's also entirely possible that a first-time castaway will earn the coveted spot.)

According to Gold Derby, fans think that Joe is most likely to return from Extinction Island. He was the first to be voted out after the merge, but since he's so good at challenges, 41% of viewers polled on the site said that they believe he'll beat the competition and re-enter the game like Rick did.

Twenty-seven percent of voters believe that Kelley will the be one to make it back, and the fact that she's so new to Extinction Island may be a positive, as she isn't as mentally and physically fatigued as the other Extinction inhabitants.

David is in third place with 17% of the vote, and Aubry is in last with 15%.
