What is Perianal Cyst?

These are fluid-filled sacs around the anus. There are several types which may arise from the skin, glands around the anus, and more rarely, certain cancers.

Treatment of Perianal Cyst

No particular medical treatment is available for perianal cysts. Complete surgical excision is the treatment of choice.
