What the Research Shows

The most robust data comes from hyaluronic acid gel injections. In a small study (Park CJ et al., 2010), men saw an average girth increase of 1.5–2.0 cm with effects lasting 6–12 months.

Surgical approaches can add length or girth, but results are highly variable and depend on surgeon skill.

Non-surgical devices produce very modest gains (0.5–1.5 cm) after consistent use for 4–6 months.

Risks and Side Effects

All procedures carry risks, but seniors may face higher complication rates due to slower healing and underlying health issues.

• Infection or abscess formation• Scarring, nodules, or uneven appearance• Loss of sensation or erectile function• Pain, bruising, or swelling• Need for repeat procedures (especially with fillers)

Health and Surgical Risk Considerations

Before any elective procedure, evaluate overall health and life expectancy. Two predictive models for serious comorbidities (though focused on liver disease) highlight the importance of risk stratification:

D'Amico G. et al. (2006): Identified factors predicting survival in liver cirrhosis. Poorer health correlates with higher complication risks.

Kamath PS & Wiesner RH (2001): MELD score helps predict post-operative mortality in end-stage liver patients. High scores signal caution for elective surgery.

Even if you don't have liver disease, these studies illustrate that structured health assessments help decide if you're a good candidate. Discuss your cardiovascular, metabolic, and clotting risks with your physician.

Non-Surgical Alternatives

If surgery or injections feel too risky, consider:

Vacuum erection devices: Improve blood flow; no permanent size change but can aid erections.

Penile traction therapy: Daily, consistent stretching may yield small gains over months.

Pelvic floor exercises: Strengthening muscles can improve erection quality and rigidity.

Counseling and sex therapy: Address performance anxiety, relationship issues, and self-esteem.

Psychological and Relationship Factors

Many men overestimate how important penis size is to partners. Communication often resolves mismatched expectations.

Anxiety about performance can worsen erectile issues. A psychological approach sometimes restores confidence without physical alteration.
