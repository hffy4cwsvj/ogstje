The Don’ts of Combining GHK-Cu with Vitamin C

Combining GHK-Cu and Vitamin C requires a cautious approach for the best results. Avoid using these ingredients simultaneously, as their direct mixing can lead to a decrease in their overall effectiveness. Copper peptides oxidize vitamin C and negate its antioxidant properties, effectively canceling out its potential benefits.

Additionally, overuse or high concentrations of GHK-Cu and Vitamin C can lead to skin irritation or sensitization, so be mindful to follow application instructions. Understanding your skin type is essential in tailoring their use to best suit your individual needs, ensuring that you enjoy the maximum benefits without adverse effects.
