Document relevant medical conditions (e.g., diabetes, thyroid issues).

Schedule a Comprehensive Evaluation

Seek a sexual medicine specialist, urogynecologist, or gynecologist with experience in female sexual dysfunction.

A thorough exam may include pelvic assessment, hormone panels, and vascular testing.

Explore First-Line Therapies

Hormonal Treatments: Topical estrogen for vaginal atrophy or systemic therapy if indicated.

Lubricants & Moisturizers: Water-based gels can immediately improve comfort.

Pelvic Floor Physical Therapy: Strengthening or relaxing muscles may enhance sensation and reduce pain.

Psychosexual Counseling: Work with a sex therapist to address anxiety, past trauma, or relationship issues.

Discuss PRP Treatment

Ask about practitioner experience, procedure protocols, and expected outcomes.

Review potential side effects: mild swelling, bruising, or temporary discomfort.

Clarify cost (often not covered by insurance) and number of sessions recommended.

Practical Tips for Managing Arousal Frustration

Prioritize "me time" to reduce stress and reconnect with your body.

Incorporate mindfulness or guided imagery to focus on physical sensations.

Share your needs: honest dialogue with your partner can foster intimacy rather than pressure.

When to Seek Further Help

If you experience any of the following, seek medical attention promptly:

Sudden pelvic pain or bleeding

Signs of infection after any procedure (fever, swelling, discharge)

Mood changes or suicidal thoughts linked to sexual distress
