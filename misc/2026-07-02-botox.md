Botox and Dysport Q&A

Botox & Dysport are ideal for adults looking to soften fine lines and wrinkles while helping to prevent new ones from forming. A quick consultation with your provider can determine if this treatment is right for your skin and goals.”

Results from botox & dysport typically last 3–4 months. Most patients begin to notice results within 3–7 days, with full effects appearing around 10–14 days after treatment. Longevity can vary depending on factors like metabolism, treatment area, and dosage. Regular maintenance treatments can help sustain results over time.

Botox & Dysport treatments require little to no downtime. Most patients return to normal activities the same day, though mild redness or swelling at the injection site may occur temporarily.
