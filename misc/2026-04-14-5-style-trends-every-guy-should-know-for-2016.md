# 5 Style Trends Every Guy Should Know For 2016

You might not pay close attention, but fashion trends are important in the making of a stylish guy. So we headed over to the New York Fashion Week shows to see where your style game should be headed.

Related: The Better Man Project, 2,476 tips to keep you happy and healthy for life

Good news: You don’t need to go full-blown Derek Zoolander to make your style a little more modern. You do need to incorporate small—but strong—details to your look.

Here are five up-and-coming trends, straight from the designers—and the most functional way to wear them.

Supersized Proportions

Loosen up, gents: It’s time to take a breather from those slim jeans.

[Should Know](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/blog/7776932301.html)

New York designers are betting on oversized pants and coats next fall. The result is contemporary and cool.

Tip: This isn’t a free pass to wear ill-fitting clothes. Your best bet for rocking this trend is to keep proportions balanced.

Translation: slim trousers with a loose-fitting top or a sleek shirt with wide-leg pants.

A Neutral Shift

[Know For](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/)

[Guy Should](https://lumina-dermis.sunsimiaosd.com/)

We call them neutrals. You call them camel, black, gray, and navy.

These colors are recurring themes for fall, which is great for two reasons.

[Style Trends Every Guy](https://jointrevive.xianfengchem.com/blog/4975428296.html)

There’s no better foundation on which to build a wardrobe than these straightforward colors. They’re masculine, timeless, and easy to combine with other shades when you want to punch things up.

Tip: Dressing in one color from head to toe not only looks clean and modern, but it also makes you look taller and leaner, too.

Functional Fabrics

This trend from the past few seasons continues to build on two well-known facts:

1. We want to feel comfortable no matter what we’re wearing.

2. Temperatures are fickle, and people are constantly traveling.

The result? Fabrics that are both light and insulating, as well as breathable and moisture-wicking.

That means lightweight layers, unstructured suits, and material that’s made to move.

Tip: Although athleisure is still a thing, a less-jarring take on the trend is clothing that blends into your weekday work attire, like breathable sport coats that won’t wrinkle.

Textured Layers

Color isn’t your only option for adding interest to your wardrobe. Wearing extreme textures—such as chunky tweeds, quilted fabrics, knits, and shearling accents—is an easy way to look stylish.

Tip: If you’re new to bold textures, try wearing just one—a jacket or scarf is your best bet.

[Guy Should Know](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/blog/9320998981.html)

Style pros can layer on varying textures, as long as they keep colors simple and complementary (e.g., shades of blue or gray).
