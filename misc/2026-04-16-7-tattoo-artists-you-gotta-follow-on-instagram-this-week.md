# 7 Tattoo Artists You Gotta Follow On Instagram This Week

When you drop big bucks on a tattoo, you want something you won't look back on and regret. Unless you've fallen victim to these common tattoo mistakes, you know that a great tattoo should be done by an artist who knows what the hell they're doing.

Luckily, Instagram is filled with tattoo artists who've mastered the art and look cool doing it. These artists probably aren't the type to write Ryan Reynolds' name on your ass, but they'll sure as hell inspire you to invest in some ink of your own or at least think about it for a long, long time.

[Tattoo Artists You](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/blog/6049543731.html)

Take a look at some of the coolest tattoo artists you should be following on Instagram right now.

Nikko Hurtado

@nikkohurtado

Where they tattoo: Black Anchor LA

Location: Los Angeles, CA

Featured on LA Ink and Tattoo Wars, Hurtado has tattooed celebrities such as Drake, The Game, and Wale. Hurtado is known for eye-catching color portraits and has created designs of everything from Daenerys Targaryen to Yoda—and, oh yeah, tattooed The Rock.

Valerie Vargas

@valeriemodernclassic

Where they tattoo: Modern Classic Tattoo

Location: London

A London-based artist since 2007, Vargas is the co-owner of Modern Classic Tattoo and specializes in large scale projects. If you're into killer full-body designs and find yourself headed to London, hit up her shop ASAP.

Scott Campbell

@scampbell333

[Artists You Gotta Follow](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/blog/3927092007.html)

[Gotta Follow On Instagram](https://insulin-pathways.hbsnzj.com/blog/9662337004.html)

Where they tattoo: Saved Tattoo

Location: Brooklyn, NY

The Williamsburg-based Saved Tattoo owner and artist has no shortage of intricate designs on his account to inspire your next ink.

Kim Saigh

@kimsaigh

Where they tattoo: Memoir Tattoo

Originally from Cleveland, Ohio, Saigh opened up Memoir Tattoo with Shawn Barber in 2009. If you're a fan of detailed animal designs and colorful floral art, follow Saigh right now.

Michael Taguet

@michaeltaguet

[You Gotta Follow On](https://beautybiometrics.hbsnzj.com/)

Where they tattoo: Yama Tattoo

Location: Saint-Chamond, France

Taguet is a skilled artist who specializes in realism and portrait designs in both color and black and white. And he can tattoo a mean Alien.

Luke Wessman

@lukewessman

Chances are you already follow Wessman. But if not, your feed is about to get a lot cooler. You might recognize Wessman from shows like Miami Ink and NY Ink. Some of his clients include actor Matt Dillon and pro baseball player CJ Wilson. If that wasn't impressive enough, the guy knows Alex Atala.

[On Instagram](https://glycometrix.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/9220770499.html)

[Gotta Follow On Instagram](https://innate-signal.jcesqc8.com/)

Angelique Houtkamp

@angeliquehoutkamp

Where they tattoo: Salon Serpent Tattoo Parlour

Location: Amsterdam

[On Instagram This Week](https://defensecascade.china-akan.com/blog/7940671481.html)

Take a look at Houtkamp's account and you'll most likely immediately find a free spot on your body for a new tattoo, whether it's a portrait or a pocketknife. Houtkamp owns Salon Serpent Tattoo Parlour, and is also known for doing nipple tattoos for breast cancer survivors. Make sure to stop by during your next trip to Amsterdam.
