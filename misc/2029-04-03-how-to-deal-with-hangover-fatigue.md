Why does alcohol cause fatigue?

Generally, the more alcohol you drink and the more dehydrated you become, the more exhausted you feel the morning after. Here’s why people get dehydrated when they consume excessive amounts of alcohol.

Alcohol is a diuretic that increases the need and frequency to urinate.

Excessive urination leads to a depletion of moisture within your body.

In addition, because you’re filling your system with alcohol, you likely aren’t drinking enough water.

Alcohol also slows down how much antidiuretic hormone (ADH) your body can produce.

You won’t be able to maintain the necessary hydration levels to function correctly.

Alcohol reduces blood sugar production and can impede your ability to fall asleep.

Excessive urination, depleted blood sugar, and dehydration will make you feel exhausted and fatigued.

Over-the-counter pain relievers are another accessible option for overcoming hangover fatigue.
