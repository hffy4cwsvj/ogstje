Which Covid tests can be used for travel?

Broadly speaking, there are two types of tests available: molecular and antigen. Most tests are administered via nasal swabs, although some may take the form of saliva or throat swabs. Regardless of which option you choose, molecular and antigen tests are designed to detect an active case of Covid-19.

[Rapid PCR For](https://innate-signal.jcesqc8.com/blog/8344967130.html)

Molecular tests such as the RT-PCR or rapid PCR are designed to detect DNA belonging to the SARS-CoV-2 virus. These are highly accurate and rarely produce incorrect results. The RT-PCR test must be processed at a lab and returns results within 72 hours of the test, while the rapid PCR test can produce same-day results.

Antigen tests detect specific proteins that coat the surface of the SARS-CoV-2 virus. These tests are less accurate than molecular tests with a 1 in 5 rate of incorrect results; however, they can return results within 15 minutes.

Different countries have differing requirements for the types of tests accepted. For example, antigen tests can satisfy requirements for entry to the US but may not be accepted by international airports. It’s important to do your research before you travel to ensure you don’t wind up stuck at an airport in the middle of your trip.
