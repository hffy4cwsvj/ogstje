Published on: 7/21/2026

What triggers lupus flare-ups and makes symptoms worse?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Lupus flare-ups occur when symptoms suddenly worsen, and they are commonly triggered by stress, infections, ultraviolet (UV) light from the sun, certain medications, and hormonal changes. Recognizing these triggers early can help you manage lupus more effectively and reduce the frequency and severity of flares.

Because lupus symptoms often mimic other conditions and vary widely from person to person, identifying what's actually causing your discomfort can be challenging. A free, instant, online symptom check can help you clarify your symptoms, understand possible causes, and guide your next steps—whether that means seeing a specialist, adjusting your lifestyle, or preparing better questions for your doctor. Taking a few minutes now could save you weeks of uncertainty.
