If you follow fitness accounts on Instagram, you've probably seen tons of videos with minibands. The small loops are a popular tool to develop the glutes, which means that anyone looking to build up a stronger, shapelier butt is likely showing off their band work—so these routines aren't typically aimed at the iron-pounding, heavy lifter types.

Dudes might be dismissive of that type of workout, but hold your judgment before you totally turn your back on the bands. There are plenty of gains to be gotten from them, especially if you're training towards a stronger lower body. Trainer Charlee Atkins, C.S.C.S., advises that anyone who doesn't skip leg day should have some band work in their repertoire, no matter their goals.
