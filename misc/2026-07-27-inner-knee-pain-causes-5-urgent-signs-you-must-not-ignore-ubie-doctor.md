Inner knee pain, also called medial knee pain, is often caused by damage to cartilage (such as a meniscus tear), inflammation of surrounding soft tissues like tendons or bursae, or nerve irritation affecting the inside of the knee. Common triggers include sports injuries, arthritis, overuse, and sudden twisting movements.

Because inner knee pain can stem from many different conditions—some minor, others requiring prompt care—identifying the underlying cause early is key to faster relief and preventing long-term damage.
