What is Anal Pruritus?

This refers to itching on the skin around the anus, where feces is passed.

Typical Symptoms of Anal Pruritus

Itching around the anus

Consuming excessive amounts of food that can irritate the gut

Hemorrhoids or lumps around the anus

Regular use of an automatic self-cleaning toilet seat

Anal pain

Bleeding from the anus

Diagnostic Questions for Anal Pruritus

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you have anal itching?

Do you eat a lot of irritating foods?

Do you have hemorrhoids or lumps around your anus?

Do you use a self-cleaning toilet seat regularly?

Are you experiencing anal pain?

Treatment of Anal Pruritus

The treatment of anal itching depends on its cause. Creams and oral medication may be helpful.
