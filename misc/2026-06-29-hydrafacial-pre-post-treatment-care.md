Pre-Treatment Instructions

Discontinue retinol use 5-7 days prior, if possible

Arrive to your appointment with clean skin

Post-Treatment Instructions

Keep your skin clean and free of heavy makeup in the following 24 hours after facial

Stick to your lightweight serums and SPF to prevent skin barrier clogging 48 hours post

Discuss skin health and at home medical grade skincare options with your esthetician

Follow-Up & Maintenance

Consistent follow-up treatments and a customized at-home skincare routine are key to maintaining and enhancing your results. At MD Esthetics, we create personalized treatment plans at the end of each visit and provide tailored product recommendations to support your skin’s ongoing health and progress.
