Cirrhosis significantly changes how the liver processes medications, making careful drug selection essential to prevent further liver damage. Many common medications—including certain pain relievers, sedatives, statins, and antibiotics—require dose adjustments or should be avoided entirely in people with cirrhosis. Acetaminophen must be limited, NSAIDs like ibuprofen are generally discouraged, and some drugs can trigger dangerous complications such as bleeding, kidney injury, or hepatic encephalopathy.

Because medication safety in cirrhosis depends on liver function, other conditions, and current symptoms, understanding your specific situation is critical.
