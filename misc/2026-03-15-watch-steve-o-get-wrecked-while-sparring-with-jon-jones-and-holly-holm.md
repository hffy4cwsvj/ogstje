Just don’t add “being an MMA fighter” to that list.

Steve-O, who is an avid MMA fan, recently visited the Jackson Wink MMA Academy in Albuquerque, New Mexico and decided it would be fun to go up against some of the best in the business. As with his infamous stunts, this went downhill extremely quick.

Steve-O first took on retired MMA fighter Jon Jones, widely considered to be one of the greatest MMA fighters ever. The match-up went about as well as you’d expect, with Steve-O flinching at every tiny move Jones made.

“I don’t like it!” Steve-O says to Jones in a clip of the spar. Jones easily tackles Steve-O to the floor as the Jackass alum laughs uncontrollably.

[Watch Steve-O](https://musclemend.sunsimiaosd.com/)

“He may be the G.O.A.T., but @jonnybones is still very lucky I went so easy on him,” Steve-O posted on Instagram, accompanied by a video of the “fight.”

Steve-O also went up against MMA fighter Holly Holm during his time at Jackson Wink—and got absolutely wrecked. In clips of their time on the mat, Holm easily overpowers Steve-O, throwing him to the ground not once but twice.

Steve-O giggles throughout both “matches,” and his maniacal laughs are filled with child-like glee. Yes, he's laughing even while he's getting the wind knocked out of him by Holm.

Someone please wrap this man in bubble wrap.
