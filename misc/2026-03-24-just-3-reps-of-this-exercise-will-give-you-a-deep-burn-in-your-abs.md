# Just 3 Reps Of This Exercise Will Give You a Deep Burn In Your Abs

There are few things in life more satisfying than a deep burn in your abs.

This exercise, the hollow-body kickout, is one of the quickest routes to that feeling—and a stronger core.

You’ll start from a hollow-body position, a challenging stance to begin with because it requires your entire core to activate at once.

If you lack strength and stability throughout your middle, you’ll feel the burn almost instantly, says Men’s Health Next Top Trainer Andy Speer, C.S.C.S.
