# 7 of Our Favorite Brands Donating Proceeds to COVID-19 Relief Efforts

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

In the wake of the COVID-19 pandemic, companies and communities are banding together in unprecedented ways.

While the crisis is taking a major toll on the fashion and retail industries, there are brands who aren’t letting those losses keep them from giving back. In fact, they’ve found a way turn transactions into donations, leading to an uptick in sales and over $200,000 raised for crisis relief: Brands x Better.

Brands x Better is a coalition of cool brands who have pledged to donate 10% of proceeds to nonprofit organizations supporting coronavirus relief efforts. Their commitment states that “the organizations we’ve chosen are focused on fighting and alleviating the social and economic impact of COVID-19.”

Nate Checketts, co-founder of Rhone, spearheaded the effort. Rhone partnered with 26 other direct-to-consumer labels to launch Brands x Better, which now has a roster of 80 brands (and counting) who’ve taken the pledge. Members include Men's Health favorites like Dagne Dover, Faherty and Greats, all of which are offering discounts in addition to giving back.

This is where you come in.

“By purchasing from companies taking part in our coalition, you are not only supporting charities fighting COVID-19, but ensuring employees and suppliers of brands can keep working,” the Brands x Better website explains.

Whether you're stocking up on loungewear or planning your post-quarantine comeback, these are high-quality, stylish pieces that you can feel good about investing in now, knowing that they have a future in your wardrobe.
