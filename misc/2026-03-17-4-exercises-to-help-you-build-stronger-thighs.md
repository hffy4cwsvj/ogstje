# 4 Exercises to Help You Build Stronger Thighs

[Exercises to](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/)

If you're working out to build thick thighs, you need to do more than just basic squats or hip adduction (think the Thigh Master) to see real results. You're goal is actually to target more than one muscle in the upper leg, so there's a range of different exercises you can do to get an effective workout. And because there are multiple muscles involved, you should be doing more than just isolation moves. Sure, leg extensions can be great depending on your goals—but especially if you're working out at home, there are probably better options.

Trainer Charlee Atkins, C.S.C.S. is well aware of the misconceptions people have about working their legs. "The muscles of the thigh: quads, hamstrings, and glutes," she says. "Compound movements (exercises that involve multiple joints and muscle groups) will always be superior for fat loss and/or muscle gain."

When you're looking to add compound movements to your workout, you don't have to immediately jump to barbells. Especially if you're training at home, you can use other implements like dumbbells, kettlebells, or whatever else you have handy to add a load. For this four exercise series, Atkins uses dumbbells with a variety of grips. If you're using other weights—or if you just want to switch things up—check out these other ways you can hold them.
