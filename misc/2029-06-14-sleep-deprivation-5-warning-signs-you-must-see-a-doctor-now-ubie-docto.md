Sleep deprivation can be troublesome, and it is important to know when to see a doctor. If you have trouble sleeping that affects your daily activities, like work or school, or if you feel tired all the time, it might be a good idea to talk to a doctor.

Here are some signs that it might be time to see a doctor:

Long-lasting sleep troubles: If you have been having trouble sleeping for more than a few weeks.
