Dear Men’s Health Style,

I need a shirt and tie and slacks to wear to a wedding rehearsal dinner this summer. I won’t need to be in a suit, but would like something bright for the summer that's fairly dressy. Please give me some ideas.

Thanks, Tony

Follow @MensHealthStyle on Twitter!

Dear Tony,

Indeed, wedding season is upon us. As if watching your best buddy make a lifelong commitment to monogamy and watching The Notebook reruns like any faithful companion should isn’t difficult enough, you need to look sharp in the summer heat. And maybe cool enough to leave with a bridesmaid.

First off, we applaud your request for a pop of color. Most guys would just keep it safe with neutrals, but the reception dinner is actually the most appropriate place for a dash of flash. The wedding is a decidedly more serious affair. Adding a bright tie is an obvious option, but here are a few others, depending on your comfort level:

The bright blazer: A well-tailored jacket in an eye-catching hue can make a bold and sophisticated statement. But choose the right shade, so you look like the guy who vacations on the Italian Riviera and not Boca. We like strong colors, like coral, cobalt and Kelly green, in lieu of pastels. Try the aforementioned cobalt blue blazer and slate gray cotton trouser with a crisp white shirt for an elegant combination.

[Rehearsal Dinner](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/blog/5516269434.html)

The summery shirt: A playful pattern and a solid knit or cotton tie is one way to look appropriate and still feel like yourself. Add a navy blazer to tone it down if you feel too showy. We’d rather show than tell, so here are our favorite shirts of the season.

Cotton trousers: If it’s a casual enough dinner, chinos may be an easy option, especially paired with the crisp cotton shirt and a tie mentioned above. Opt for a slim, sleek fit—and break out of the khaki-colored slump. Dockers, Banana Republic and J. Crew have colors that range from Nantucket red to lagoon blue. Or, consider one of these lightweight suits.

The pocket square: If you end up losing steam on any of the color ideas mentioned above, add some flair with a colorful, patterned pocket square. The added bonuses: you can skip the tie, add some color AND get full credit for minding the elegant details. Women notice these things, as well as a few grooming details you shouldn’t overlook.

Finally, it goes without saying that no matter what option you choose, breathable fabrics like cotton or linen are your best bets to feeling easy-breezy, even after your eighth glass of champagne. Maybe even after doing repeated fist pumps to Billy Idol’s “White Wedding.”

Good luck!

—The Men’s Health Style Team
