Explanation

Sleep problems can come from many different things. First, changes in hormones can make it hard to sleep. This is because hormones help control when we feel sleepy or awake.

Second, your body temperature can also play a role. If your body is too hot or too cold, it might wake you up at night.

Lastly, stress or bad habits, like using screens before bed, can keep you from sleeping well.
