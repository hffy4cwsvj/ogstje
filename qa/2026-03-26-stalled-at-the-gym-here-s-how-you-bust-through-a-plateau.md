# Stalled at the Gym? Here’s How You Bust Through a Plateau

[Bust Through](https://glycometrix.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/3597161387.html)

If you hit the gym all the time, it’s extremely frustrating when you haven’t added strength or muscle in months and your gains come to a screeching halt.

The fastest way to bust through your plateau: Own your numbers.

In the higher-rep sets, most guys don’t use a heavy enough weight.

[Here How](https://musclemend.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/8388223423.html)

(Now that you have strength and muscle, burn the fat on top with THE 21-DAY METASHRED. One guy lost 25 pounds of fat in 6 weeks!)

Warm up, pick a safe exercise—like the dumbbell bench press or dumbbell split squat—and then grab a weight you think you can lift 10 times.

Now go for broke: If you really push, you’ll not only meet but probably exceed your repetition goal. Surprising right?

From there, select an exercise for every body part and try lifting various loads until you find your true 10-rep max weight for each move.
