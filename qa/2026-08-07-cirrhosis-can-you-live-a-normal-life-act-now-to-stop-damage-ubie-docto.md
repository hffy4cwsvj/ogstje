Can someone live a normal life after being diagnosed with cirrhosis of the liver?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Cirrhosis is a serious liver condition that scars healthy tissue, affecting how your liver functions. While living with cirrhosis can be challenging, many people maintain a good quality of life with proper management, including medication, lifestyle changes, and regular monitoring. However, cirrhosis often leads to complications such as fatigue, fluid buildup, and increased infection risk, which can make daily life unpredictable.
