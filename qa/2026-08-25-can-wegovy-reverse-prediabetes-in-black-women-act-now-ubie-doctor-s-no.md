Doctor's Note: Can Wegovy Reverse Prediabetes in Black Women Before It's Too Late?

Prediabetes affects millions of Americans, and Black women face a disproportionately higher risk of progressing to type 2 diabetes. With the FDA's approval of Wegovy (semaglutide) for weight management, many are asking: can Wegovy reverse prediabetes in Black women and lower their diabetes risk? Here's what current evidence and expert opinion tell us.

Why Black Women Have a Higher Diabetes Risk

Black women develop type 2 diabetes at younger ages and with fewer warning signs than other groups. Contributing factors include:

Genetic predisposition to insulin resistance

Higher rates of obesity (a key driver of insulin resistance)

Socioeconomic barriers to healthy food and safe exercise spaces

Limited access to preventive healthcare

Chronic stress and its impact on blood sugar control

What Is Wegovy?

Wegovy is the brand name for semaglutide at a 2.4 mg weekly dose, approved for chronic weight management in adults with obesity or overweight plus at least one weight-related condition (like prediabetes). Semaglutide belongs to a class of medications called GLP-1 receptor agonists, which:

Mimic a hormone (GLP-1) that regulates appetite and blood sugar

Slow stomach emptying, helping you feel full longer

Enhance insulin release when blood sugar is high

Reduce the release of glucagon (a hormone that raises blood sugar)

Originally approved for type 2 diabetes under lower doses, Wegovy's higher dose has demonstrated average weight loss of 15% over 68 weeks in clinical trials.

Can Wegovy Reverse Prediabetes?

Direct studies on Wegovy reversing prediabetes specifically in Black women are not yet available. However, we can piece together relevant data:

Weight Loss and Diabetes Prevention• In landmark studies like the Diabetes Prevention Program (DPP), losing 5–7% of body weight cut progression to diabetes by 58%.• Wegovy's robust weight loss (often >10%) suggests an even greater potential to improve blood sugar control and insulin sensitivity.

GLP-1 Agonists and Glucose Control• Semaglutide lowers fasting and post-meal glucose levels by boosting insulin when needed.• Smaller studies in prediabetic adults using GLP-1 drugs (e.g., liraglutide) showed improvements in glycemic markers and fewer conversions to diabetes over 1–2 years.

Racial and Ethnic Considerations• Pharmacologic response to semaglutide appears similar across racial groups.• Black women are underrepresented in many trials, but subgroup analyses haven't flagged major differences in efficacy or safety.

Bottom line: While no trial focuses exclusively on Wegovy reversing prediabetes in Black women, the strong weight loss and glucose-lowering effects of semaglutide are promising for reducing diabetes risk when combined with lifestyle changes.
