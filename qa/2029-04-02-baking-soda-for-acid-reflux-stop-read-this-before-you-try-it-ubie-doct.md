Baking soda is not a safe home remedy for acid reflux. While it may temporarily neutralize stomach acid, improper use can cause serious health risks, including sodium overload, electrolyte imbalances, stomach rupture in rare cases, and dangerous interactions with medications. It also isn't a long-term solution and can mask symptoms of more serious underlying conditions like GERD, ulcers, or hiatal hernia.

If you're experiencing frequent heartburn or reflux, it's important to understand what's actually causing it rather than relying on risky quick fixes.
