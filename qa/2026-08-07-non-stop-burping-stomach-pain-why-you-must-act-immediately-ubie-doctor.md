What could be the cause of non-stop burping with stomach pain?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Non-stop burping accompanied by stomach pain is often caused by swallowing excess air (aerophagia) or an underlying issue such as gallstones, acid reflux, or gastritis. Aerophagia can result from eating too quickly, chewing gum, or drinking carbonated beverages, while gallstones may trigger sharp upper-abdominal pain and excessive belching after meals.
