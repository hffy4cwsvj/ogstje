What might cause sharp breast pain, and how should it be addressed?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Sharp breast pain is often caused by conditions such as scapulothoracic bursitis (inflammation between the shoulder blade and rib cage) or cervical radiculopathy (a pinched nerve in the neck). Other possible causes include hormonal changes, muscle strain, or costochondritis. Because the cause can vary widely, seeing a doctor is essential to get an accurate diagnosis and effective treatment.

Explanation

Sharp breast pain can come from different sources. One possible cause is scapulothoracic bursitis, which is when the area near the shoulder blade becomes inflamed. This inflammation can lead to pain that feels like it's coming from the breast.

[Note Ubie](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/)

Another cause can be cervical radiculopathy, which happens when nerves in the neck are pinched or irritated. This can send pain signals to the breast area.

Here are some steps to address sharp breast pain:

See a Doctor: It's important to visit a healthcare provider. They can help figure out what's causing the pain.

Get Tests: The doctor may do tests like X-rays or MRIs to see what's going on inside your body.

Follow Treatment Plans: Depending on the cause, treatment could include physical therapy, medications, or other therapies.
