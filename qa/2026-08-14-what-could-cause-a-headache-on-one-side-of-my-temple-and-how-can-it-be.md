Explanation

Headaches that occur on one side of the temple can have a variety of causes. One possible cause is cluster headaches, which are headaches that often occur on one side of the head and can include symptoms like eye redness and tearing.

[cause headache on one](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/blog/0860718702.html)

Another potential cause of headaches on one side of the temple is temporomandibular joint syndrome, which lead to pain around the temple as well as other muscles used when chewing food. Stress and teeth grinding can contribute to temporomandibular joint syndrome.

While giant cell arteritis is rare, it is an important diagnosis to consider especially for anyone age 50 years and older. Giant cell arteritis is caused by inflammation of the blood vessels in the area of the temples.

Medications and other therapies are available to treat disorders that cause headaches on one side of the temple. The most appropriate treatment for you will depend on the exact cause of your symptoms, so you should consult a healthcare provider for a personalized medical assessment and treatment plan.
