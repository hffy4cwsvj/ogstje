Explanation

There are several simple ways to manage elbow pain at night. Consider using a pillow to support your arm—placing it under or next to your elbow may prevent excessive bending. This support can reduce pressure on any painful areas and may help ease discomfort. Many find relief by sleeping on the side opposite to their injured elbow. Try gradually adjusting your sleep position until you find what feels most comfortable. These adjustments let your elbow rest naturally and can improve sleep—which your body needs for healing.
