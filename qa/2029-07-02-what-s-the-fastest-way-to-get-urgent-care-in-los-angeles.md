What is a house call?

A house call is when a physician will come to your home or location to provide you with care. This is an excellent option for those who may struggle with transportation or may not drive themselves. It can also benefit those who have mobility issues and find going out into the community exhausting.

While urgent care facilities are a great option, these are not always feasible for patients or families. In-home care can allow families to have a physician look at several family members, cutting down on the amount of time needed to take off work. Home calls often save patients money, time, and driving costs and are more convenient.
