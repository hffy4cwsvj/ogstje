Research indicates that consuming approximately 2 grams of ginger per day may help reduce blood sugar levels, particularly in people with type 2 diabetes. Ginger contains bioactive compounds that may improve insulin sensitivity and support glucose metabolism. However, results vary, and ginger should not replace prescribed treatments. Always consult a healthcare provider before adding any supplement to your routine, especially if you take blood sugar or blood-thinning medications.
