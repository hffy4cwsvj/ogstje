Internal Medicine

Melatonin is a hormone that helps regulate the sleep-wake cycle and can be effective for treating certain sleep disorders. It works best in adults over 55 and in people with disrupted circadian rhythms, such as those with jet lag, shift work sleep disorder, or delayed sleep phase syndrome. By signaling to the body that it's time to rest, melatonin can improve sleep onset and overall sleep quality.

Explanation

Melatonin is a hormone that helps regulate sleep and wake cycles. According to the provided references, melatonin can be beneficial for sleep disorders, particularly in older adults.

Use in Older Adults: One study highlights that melatonin is effective in treating insomnia in people aged 55 and older. This is because melatonin production naturally decreases with age, and supplementation can help restore balance.

Circadian Rhythm Disorders: Another study explains that melatonin is useful for people with disrupted sleep patterns, such as those caused by shift work or jet lag. It helps by adjusting the body's internal clock to align with the desired sleep-wake schedule.
